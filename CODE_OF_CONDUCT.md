# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), v2.1.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion constructive.
Harassment or discrimination of any kind is not tolerated. Report concerns
privately to the maintainers (see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) for a private
contact path). Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and
comments that violate this policy.
